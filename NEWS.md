## Lead story

**1. HUANG IS BACK — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO (announced Monday 23 Nov; Tuesday fallout)**
- Reuters / AP / Nvidia press release, 23 Nov 2026: Nvidia's board
  voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate co-founder Jensen Huang as
  president, chief executive and board director, effective immediately.
  Huang resigned on 12 Nov, 11 days earlier, after reports of
  extramarital affairs, including one with Alice Huang, a former
  executive at Singapore-based cloud firm Megaspeed whom US officials
  suspect of ties to Chinese intelligence; at least two further affairs
  with Nvidia employees were also alleged. The board had formally ruled
  out his return on 18 Nov, then reversed itself six days later.
- Reuters / CNBC, 23 Nov 2026: shares jumped about 33% in Monday
  trading (closing near $165.57, up from Friday's $125.53 close; about
  +31.5% on the day), adding roughly $1 trillion in market value; the
  Nasdaq Composite rose about 7%. Shares had closed at $213.66 on
  29 Oct, before the scandal broke. President Trump told CNBC on Monday:
  "I spoke to them... And I convinced them. I made the deal." The board
  said it received input from senior levels of the US government and was
  confident the return posed no national security risk.
- Bloomberg Law / AP / Engadget, 23 Nov 2026: Senate Banking Committee
  chair Sen. Tim Scott announced on Monday that Huang will testify
  publicly before the panel on 3 December about export controls, after
  closed-door interviews with three House committees on 1 December.
  Nvidia's own investigation (led by former federal prosecutor Joon Kim)
  continues; the company is appointing a Chief Compliance Officer.
- Tuesday 24 Nov fallout: SK hynix's US-traded shares rose 20% after
  Huang's reinstatement; "Trump backs Nvidia boss's return but top
  Democrat vows probe" led Tuesday news bulletins.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF: the coordinator's brief said the +33% surge
happened on Tuesday 24 Nov. It did not — every source dates the
reinstatement announcement and the share surge to Monday 23 Nov. The
image therefore labels the lead "ANNOUNCED MONDAY" and shows Tuesday
only as fallout (chip-stock rally, congressional probe vows).

## Story panels

2. **Libya — Kyiv says it sank Russian warship (attack Sunday, confirmed Monday).**
   AP / Reuters / United24, 23 Nov 2026: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy
   said on Monday that Ukrainian forces deployed seven domestically made
   naval drones on Sunday and sank the Russian corvette Merkury
   (commissioned 2023, Black Sea Fleet) off Benghazi, Libya; a Ka-27
   combat helicopter aboard was destroyed and the dry cargo ship
   MV Angara, which Kyiv says Moscow used to transport weapons, was hit.
   The UN called for an independent investigation. Neither Moscow nor
   Tripoli has confirmed Ukraine's role. (The 23 Nov edition covered the
   strike itself; this edition's news is Zelenskyy's Monday confirmation.)

3. **Europe — EU proposes 22nd sanctions package (Monday 23 Nov).**
   European Commission / Reuters, 23 Nov 2026: Commission President
   Ursula von der Leyen presented the proposed 22nd package on Monday:
   a transaction ban extending to about 60 Russian banks, all to be
   removed from SWIFT; a crackdown on crypto and payment networks plus a
   new Crypto Sanctions Task Force; an expanded energy-services ban
   (refineries, electricity); 70+ more shadow-fleet ships listed and a
   proposed ban on Russian-insured tankers; a visa ban for Russian
   ex-combatants; a ban on Russian teams and athletes competing under the
   Russian flag at EU sports events. The package is a proposal — all 27
   member states must agree before it takes effect.

4. **India–Nepal — $1.62bn lifeline for flood-hit Nepal (Monday 23 Nov).**
   Reuters / AP, 23 Nov 2026: India announced on Monday a $1.62bn
   economic package for flood-hit Nepal — a $377m grant for
   post-disaster reconstruction and new infrastructure, plus a $1.24bn
   line of credit for energy projects — after talks between PM Narendra
   Modi and Nepali PM Balendra Shah in New Delhi. The 26 Aug floods,
   caused by a glacier collapse, killed about 1,500 people, left 5,200
   missing and presumed dead, and caused an estimated $4.7bn in losses.

5. **Gaza — strike kills 3 in Khan Younis (Tuesday 24 Nov).**
   Reuters, 24 Nov 2026: an Israeli airstrike killed at least three
   Palestinians on Tuesday near a police outpost in Khan Younis in the
   southern Gaza Strip, local health officials said; five others were
   wounded. At least 1,600 Palestinians and five Israeli soldiers have
   been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire, per respective tallies.
   Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK jointly called on Tuesday for
   immediate, unrestricted humanitarian access to flooded Gaza.

6. **Sport — 2031 Women's World Cup to US & Mexico (Tuesday 24 Nov).**
   FIFA / news wires, 24 Nov 2026: FIFA confirmed the 2031 Women's World
   Cup will be hosted by the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and
   Jamaica; the 2035 edition goes to the United Kingdom.

7. **Gulf — Iran claims US drone downed; US sanctions widen (Tuesday 24 Nov).**
   News wires, 24 Nov 2026: Iran's army said on Tuesday it shot down a
   US drone (reported as a "Lucas" drone) over the Gulf. The US Treasury
   sanctioned more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses and front firms
   under "Operation Economic Outcast". Both items are claims/reports, not
   independently confirmed events.
